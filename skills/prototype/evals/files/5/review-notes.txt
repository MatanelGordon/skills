Orders page layouts - review session, 19 November 2026

Who: 6 support agents, one at a time. Each tried all three variants, in a
different order each time.
Task given: "One order on this page is late. Find it and tell me the customer."

Variant A (dense table, what we have today)
  2 of 6 found it in under 10 seconds. Median 21 s. Two scrolled past it.

Variant B (late orders pinned in a band on top)
  6 of 6 found it in under 10 seconds. Median 4 s.

Variant C (one card per order)
  3 of 6 found it in under 10 seconds. Median 12 s.
  "Too much scrolling" (said by 3 people).

Afterwards all six said they preferred B.
Asked for: keep the column sorting that A has.
Decision in the room: go with B.
