# Checkout usability test, round 2

Six participants, moderated, on a laptop. Task: buy the cedar planter box.

## What we saw

- 6 of 6 reached the payment step without help.
- 4 of 6 paused for more than ten seconds before pressing the last button.
- 2 of 6 scrolled back up to the summary before paying.

## What they told us afterwards

- 5 of 6 said the steps were "easy" or "obvious".
- 3 of 6 used the word "dodgy", "sketchy" or "untrustworthy".

## Things said at the last step

- "Why is it 52.99 now? It said 48."
- "Is the basket the same thing as the bag?"
- "I never asked for protection."
- "Continue to what? Does that charge me?"

## Not collected

Analytics, drop-off rate, A/B data. We do not know yet which of these things matters most.
