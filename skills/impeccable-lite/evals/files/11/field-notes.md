# Dispatch console: what we know

## What marketing asked for

For the product video and the trial sign-up flow, marketing wants the console to
"look simpler": show three toolbar controls instead of ten, move the other seven
into a `More` menu, and put one large green `Dispatch now` button at the top of
the screen.

## What we observed

Two shifts, six dispatchers, sitting beside them:

- Every dispatcher used at least eight of the ten toolbar tools within an hour.
  Which eight differed from person to person.
- Five of the six pressed the single-key shortcuts and rarely clicked the toolbar.
- At peak a dispatcher handles about 40 loads an hour.
- There is no single "dispatch" step. A load goes out once a driver is assigned
  and the load is released.

## What people told us

- Two dispatchers in their first week said the toolbar "looked like a lot".
- The four experienced dispatchers said they would not want anything moved.

## What we have not measured

Error rate, time per load, and whether new hires are slower because of the toolbar.
