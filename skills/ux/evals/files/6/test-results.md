# Checkout redesign: usability test, round 2

15 participants, recruited from existing customers. None had seen the redesign
and none work for us. Each tried the three tasks below on their own. The
moderator gave one hint, and only after a participant had been stuck for two
minutes.

| Task | Completed without a hint | Completed after a hint | Gave up | Median time, round 2 | Median time, round 1 | Ease rating after the task (SEQ, 1 = very difficult, 7 = very easy), mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 Add two items to the basket | 15 | 0 | 0 | 38 s | 41 s | 6.5 |
| T2 Pay for the order | 10 | 2 | 3 | 187 s | 121 s | 4.1 |
| T3 Change the delivery address after ordering | 13 | 1 | 1 | 64 s | 70 s | 5.9 |

Errors on T2: 11 in total. The participant corrected 7 of them. The other 4
were never corrected: the card form rejected a card number typed with spaces,
showed "Invalid input", and the participant stopped.

SUS questionnaire, filled in at the end of the session: mean 81.5
(lowest 60, highest 97.5).

Team summary written for Friday's go/no-go meeting:
"SUS 81.5%. Users love the new checkout. Ship."
