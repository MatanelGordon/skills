Integration notes
-----------------

This skill is independently written. It uses seven source-hashed PSTACK
principles as design evidence for behavioral tests, executable proof, native
tools, staged verification, boundary discipline, result modeling, and isolated
parallel work. No PSTACK skill, agent, script, runtime, or prompt is vendored.
The local protocol, adapters, commands, and prose are original and remain MIT.

The opt-in provenance check compares the recorded blob IDs with PSTACK's
current `main` branch so upstream drift causes a fresh human review rather than
silently changing this skill.
